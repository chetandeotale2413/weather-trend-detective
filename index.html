OUR METHOD

From satellite signal to environmental insight

01 · Observe
Collect NASA Earth-observation products and event reports.
02 · Compare
Examine locations, dates and indicator changes using transparent calculations.
03 · Explain
Translate patterns into evidence-based clues and useful next questions.
Science note: NASA POWER provides gridded/reanalysis and satellite-derived parameters, not necessarily ground-station measurements at the exact pin. A trend in one indicator alone does not prove its cause. Validate conclusions with additional datasets and local sources.
